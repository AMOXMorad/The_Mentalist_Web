## 🩸 Overview

**The Mentalist Tracker** transforms the experience of watching the hit TV series *The Mentalist* into an engaging investigation. Instead of browsing a passive episode list, you step into the mind of CBI consultant **Patrick Jane** to hunt down his arch-nemesis, the serial killer known as **Red John**.

Designed from the ground up to be **completely spoiler-free**, the dashboard intelligently reveals clues, suspects, and case evidence only as you watch each episode.

---

## 🌟 Key Features (100% Spoiler-Free)

### 🛡️ 1. Zero-Spoiler Protection Engine
- **Progression-Based Visibility:** All clues, crime symbols, suspect details, and mystery resolutions remain obscured until you verify that you've watched an episode.
- **Fast Catch-Up Picker:** Jump straight to where you currently are in the series (e.g., Season 3 Episode 10) with one click—all preceding episodes are marked as watched without spoiling future plot points.

### 📌 2. Interactive Suspect Board
- **Examine & Eliminate:** Track the official 7 suspects on a virtual investigative corkboard. Review forensic details, cross-examine clues, and cross off suspects you determine to be innocent.
- **Accusation Deadline:** Lock in your ultimate deduction before the investigative window shuts. See if your mental acuity rivals Patrick Jane’s.

### 📈 3. Dynamic Investigation Curve
- A responsive, animated progress bar that reflects the tension, momentum, and twists of Jane's journey across all 7 seasons.

### 🕸️ 4. Clue Graph & Criminal Syndicate Network
- An interactive relationship graph mapping the intricate web of forensic clues, crime patterns, and shadowy accomplices associated with the notorious Blake Association.

### 🏆 5. Detective Badges & Easter Eggs
- Unlock humorous, lore-faithful achievements celebrating Patrick Jane's legendary quirks:
  - 🥪 **The Sandwich Thief:** Commemorating Jane boldly snatching Teresa Lisbon's sandwich in hallway transit.
  - 🍵 **Toxic Tea Trip:** The cautionary tale of drinking unverified herbal tea at an active crime scene!
  - 🐯 **Tiger Tiger:** Historic milestones quoting William Blake's dark poetry.
  - 🎮 **4 Kills only Wins:** Playful tactical trolling on the grand schemes of Red John.

### 🎬 6. Adaptive Atmospheric Styling
- An evolving noir visual design: dark crimson-and-black aesthetic that immerses you in the psychological tone of the series.

### 🌍 7. Complete Bilingual Localization
- Native, switchable bilingual support:
  - **Arabic (RTL):** Rendered with the clean, modern **Cairo** typography.
  - **English (LTR):** Crisp, readable layout tuned for high contrast.

### 💾 8. Local-First & Zero Sign-Up
- All your progress is stored privately in your browser (`localStorage`).
- Backup and restore your investigation anytime via instant JSON **Export / Import**.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Engineering

This project is built using modern, production-grade frontend technologies:

- **Framework:** [Next.js 15](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, Server Components, and Turbopack bundler)
- **Language:** [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) for strict type safety
- **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) with custom dark/peace mode tokens and glassmorphism
- **Animations:** [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/) for fluid modals, transitions, and toast alerts
- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Hosting & CI/CD:** [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) Edge Network
